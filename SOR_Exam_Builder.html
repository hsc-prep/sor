SOR1 Build an Exam

Assemble a printable, full 50-mark practice exam — Multiple Choice, Question 11, a Buddhism section and a Judaism essay — ready to print or save as a PDF.

How to use this: this builds a full 50-mark practice paper: 10 Multiple Choice (10 marks, 5 per printed page) + Question 11 (5 marks, writing lines provided) + a Buddhism section (3 parts, 15 marks) + a Judaism essay (20 marks) — Buddhism and Judaism are answered in a separate writing booklet, so no lines are printed for them. Pick your options below (or leave a section on "Random pick" for a fresh combination each time you build), click "Build exam", then use your browser's Print (Ctrl/Cmd+P) and choose "Save as PDF" as the destination. The title page is page 1, Multiple Choice starts on page 2 and runs to page 3, Question 11 is page 4, the Buddhism section follows, and the Judaism essay is last.
